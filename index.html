Introduction

I was introduced to Harry Potter on my seventh birthday in 2002 when I recieved two VHS copies of Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone.

From there I began to obsess over the movies and began to read the books when I was in third grade.

Books in Order

Here are the HP (Harry Potter) books in order and release dates:

Note these are the US release dates and name.

  1. Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone (September 1, 1998)
  2. Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets (June 2, 1999)
  3. Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban (September 8, 1999)
  4. Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire (July 8, 2000)
  5. Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix (June 21, 2003)
  6. Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince (July 16, 2005)
  7. Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows (July 21, 2007)
